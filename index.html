ABOUT ME

Hey there! 👋

If you've stumbled across my portfolio, then you're probably a recruiter... or someone who somehow randomly typed this page into their browser (which raises concerns). Either way, welcome! I think you're lucky to have found this little corner of the internet. 😉

I'm El Yazid—Yazid for short—a Moroccan guy who's still trying to understand how humanity managed to build such an astonishing civilization. Out of everything we've created, computers fascinate me the most.

I've had one since I was a kid, but back then I didn't care how it worked—as long as my games launched, I was happy. Then I grew up, took my first computer science classes, and something clicked. I stopped seeing computers as magical black boxes and started seeing them for what they really are: one of humanity's greatest engineering achievements.

The deeper I dive, the more I admire the generations of engineers, researchers, and curious minds who built the foundations we stand on today. Every abstraction I peel back only makes me appreciate the elegance hidden underneath. The more I learn, the more respect I have for this craft.

That's why software engineering felt like the obvious path for me. Not just because I enjoy writing code, but because I'm fascinated by the idea that, with nothing more than curiosity, a laptop, and a few lines of code, we can create things that millions of people use, remember, or simply enjoy—from operating systems and life-saving software to games like Call of Duty.

And I'm sure, dear reader, if you're a software engineer, you know exactly what I'm talking about. So, enough about me—have a look around. I hope you enjoy exploring what I've been building as much as I've enjoyed building it.
